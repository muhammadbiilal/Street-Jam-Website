This policy describes the Street Jam app as currently built. It is updated whenever an SDK or online feature is added.

What stays on your device

Game progress, settings, unlocked skins and the current level attempt are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Advertising

This build shows no ads. It contains no analytics, crash-reporting, account or payment SDKs, and it does not request network access or the advertising ID. Game progress, settings and unlocked skins stay on your device. If a later version adds optional rewarded ads, this policy will be updated first.

Accounts and purchases

Street Jam has no accounts or login and no in-app purchases.
