Privacy policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

This policy describes the Street Jam app as currently built. It is updated whenever an SDK or online feature is added.

What stays on your device

Game progress, settings, unlocked skins and the current level attempt are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Advertising

The app can show optional rewarded ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob). An ad plays only when you choose to watch one in the garage to unlock a cosmetic vehicle skin (two ads per skin). There are no banner, interstitial or app-open ads. Ads are requested as child-directed, limited to content rated G and never personalised, and the app does not request the advertising ID permission. Game progress, settings and unlocked skins stay on your device. When an ad is requested, Google may collect and use your IP address, device information, ad interactions and diagnostics to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and keep its services secure. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your ad privacy choices through Google's User Messaging Platform, and you can review them again from the garage ("Ad privacy options") where offered. Declining ads never limits levels, hints or undo. Learn more in Google's explanation of how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Data the app does not collect itself

The app has no accounts, no purchases and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of its own.

Accounts and purchases

Street Jam has no accounts or login and no in-app purchases.

Children

Street Jam is a puzzle game for everyone, including children. It has no accounts, chat, purchases or links to social media, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. When ads are offered, they are requested as child-directed, limited to content rated G and never personalised. If you believe a child's information has been collected, contact us and it will be deleted.
