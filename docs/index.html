Clear the street, one vehicle at a time.

Street Jam is a traffic puzzle with bikes, tuk-tuks, cars and colourful decorated trucks. Look ahead, find a clear route and choose the right departure order to untangle the jam.

Coming soon to Google PlayHow to play

Android. The whole campaign works without an internet connection.

Different vehicles, different challenges

Bikes fit through narrow lanes, while cars and long trucks need more space. Follow the marked routes and make room for vehicles that cannot leave yet.

Bikes

Narrow bike lanes are theirs alone. A bike can slip out where nothing else fits.

Tuk-tuks

Rickshaws follow tight marked turns, so their whole route matters.

Cars

Cars need a wider clear lane and a longer run to the exit.

Decorated trucks

Long, colourful trucks need the entire path clear, tail included.

See your route before you drive

Tap a vehicle to preview its route, including turns. Tap again to send it on its way. If another vehicle blocks the route, the game shows what is in the way.

There is no timer rushing your decisions. Use undo, restart and hints when you want another approach.

Read the full guide

Day and night

Switch between daylight and night mode, with headlights that follow the vehicles as they turn.

Larger boards support zooming and panning so you can inspect the traffic.

Offline campaign, optional cosmetic extras

Play campaign puzzles without an internet connection, in layouts set around courtyards, truck yards and busy market streets.

Four vehicle skins are free to use. More skins are cosmetic extras that a later version may unlock with optional rewarded ads. Levels, hints and undo never depend on ads.

Questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. The campaign works without an internet connection. The whole campaign works without an internet connection.

Which devices does it run on?

Street Jam is built for Android phones and tablets.

Are there ads?

The current build has no ads. Optional rewarded ads for cosmetic skins may be offered in a later version; any change will be described in the store listing and in this site's privacy policy before it ships.

Do I need an account?

No. Progress is saved on your device.
