How to play

Every board is a traffic jam. Send each vehicle out the way its arrow points, in an order that never leaves anything stuck.

  1. Read the arrows. Each vehicle has an arrow that shows the direction it drives.
  2. Tap to preview. Tap a vehicle to see its whole route, including every turn.
  3. Tap again to drive. If the route is clear the vehicle leaves. If not, the game shows what is in the way.
  4. Mind the vehicle type. Bikes use narrow lanes, tuk-tuks take tight turns, and cars and long trucks need more room.
  5. Take your time. There is no timer. Undo and restart whenever you like, and use a hint if you are stuck.
  6. Look around. On larger boards, pinch to zoom and drag to pan.

Levels, hints and undo never depend on ads. The whole campaign works without an internet connection.
